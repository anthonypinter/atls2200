Class progress

Each cell is the share of a lesson's work done: sections read (and, for Play, Try it, Practice and Check yourself, used), quizzes finished and exercises passed. ✓ means the student marked the lesson complete. A section counts as read after enough active time for its length; used means the student changed playground controls, ran edited code, typed in an editor, pressed Check or answered the whole quiz. Reading time counts only while the page is visible and the student is active. ⚑ flags a pass after under 2 minutes in the lesson, or 5+ Checks without a pass. Students' browsers report this data, so treat it as a signal, not proof.
