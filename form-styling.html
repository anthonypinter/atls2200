Styling Forms

Browser-default forms look cramped and inconsistent. A handful of rules fixes most of it. Add them one layer at a time and watch the same form improve.

Key ideas

  • Inputs, selects and buttons don't inherit fonts. Start every form with font: inherit.
  • Put labels above inputs. It's easiest to scan and works on narrow screens.
  • accent-color recolors checkboxes, radios and range sliders in one line.
  • :user-invalid only shows errors after someone has interacted with a field, not as soon as the page loads.

Common mistakes

  • Removing the focus outline and replacing it with nothing.
  • width: 100% plus padding without box-sizing: border-box, so inputs poke out of their container.
  • Showing errors in red only. Add a text message too, for people who can't tell the colors apart.
  • Tiny checkboxes and buttons that are hard to tap on a phone.
