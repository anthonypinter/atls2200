Video & Audio

The <video> and <audio> elements play media without any plugins. Their attributes control what visitors see and whether anything plays by itself. This player is a simulation that follows the same rules as a real one.

Captions

The captions file

Captions live in a separate WebVTT text file, linked with <track>. Each cue has a start time, an end time and the text. The current cue is highlighted while the video plays.

Key ideas

  • Always include controls unless you build your own play button. Without it, people can't pause or adjust volume.
  • Browsers block autoplay with sound. Autoplay only works with muted, and should be rare.
  • List more than one <source> (like MP4 and WebM) and the browser plays the first one it supports.
  • Add captions for videos and a transcript for audio, for people who can't hear it and everyone watching on mute.
  • <audio controls> works the same way, without the picture.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading a huge video file straight from a phone. Compress it, or host it on YouTube or Vimeo and embed it.
  • Expecting autoplay to work without muted.
  • No poster, so the player shows a black box until it loads.
  • Background videos that loop forever with no way to pause them.
