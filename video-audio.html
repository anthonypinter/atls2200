Video & Audio

The <video> and <audio> elements play media without any plugins. Their attributes control what visitors see and whether anything plays by itself. This player is a simulation that follows the same rules as a real one.

Media without plugins

Browsers play video and audio on their own, with two elements that work almost the same way:

<video controls poster="mesa-cover.jpg" width="1280" height="720">
  <source src="mesa.webm" type="video/webm">
  <source src="mesa.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  <track kind="captions" src="mesa.vtt" srclang="en" label="English">
</video>

controls adds the play button, timeline and volume. Leave it out and visitors can't pause or mute, so always include it unless you build your own. poster shows an image until the video starts, instead of a black box. And listing more than one <source> lets the browser play the first format it supports.

You've probably noticed videos that start silently, or not at all. Browsers block autoplay with sound, because nobody wants a page to start talking at them. Autoplay only works when the video is also muted, and even then, use it sparingly. A background video that loops forever with no way to stop it is distracting, and a problem for people with motion sensitivity.

Captions live in a separate text file in WebVTT format, linked with <track>. They're essential for people who can't hear the audio, and useful for everyone watching on mute in a quiet room. For audio-only content, like a podcast, provide a transcript.

<audio controls> works the same way, without the picture.

Tip

Video files are big. Compress them before uploading, or host long videos on YouTube or Vimeo and embed them instead.

Key ideas

  • Always include controls unless you build your own play button. Without it, people can't pause or adjust volume.
  • Browsers block autoplay with sound. Autoplay only works with muted, and should be rare.
  • List more than one <source> (like MP4 and WebM) and the browser plays the first one it supports.
  • Add captions for videos and a transcript for audio, for people who can't hear it and everyone watching on mute.
  • <audio controls> works the same way, without the picture.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading a huge video file straight from a phone. Compress it, or host it on YouTube or Vimeo and embed it.
  • Expecting autoplay to work without muted.
  • No poster, so the player shows a black box until it loads.
  • Background videos that loop forever with no way to pause them.
