Lists

Key ideas

  • <ul> when order doesn't matter, <ol> when it does, <dl> for terms and their descriptions.
  • A list's only direct children are <li>s (or <dt>/<dd> in a dl).
  • To nest a list, put the inner list inside an <li>, after its text.
  • Navigation menus are usually a <ul> inside a <nav>, with bullets removed.

Common mistakes

  • Putting a nested <ul> directly inside the outer <ul> instead of inside an <li>.
  • Typing your own numbers or dashes into list items.
  • Removing bullets but not the default padding-left, leaving a mysterious indent.
  • Using <br> between lines instead of real list items.
