What you've covered

This part was about one page working on every screen: layouts that change at breakpoints, images that fit and download efficiently, and sizes that grow smoothly.

  • Media Queries: min-width queries add rules on wider screens, and later queries win when several apply.
  • Responsive Images: max-width: 100% and height: auto keep images inside their containers; srcset offers smaller files.
  • Picture & Lazy Loading: sizes tells the browser how wide an image will be, picture swaps the crop, and lazy loading waits.
  • Mobile-First Workflow: Write the phone layout first, outside any query, then add for wider screens.
  • Fluid Sizing: clamp(), min(), max() and calc() let sizes grow smoothly within limits.
