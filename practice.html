Scored practice

Exercises & Quizzes

One set a week, on that week's Core Web Development lessons. These are new problems, not the ones from the lessons, and they're scored.

How the sets are scored

  • Exercises: 5 points each. Check your work as often as you like. Your best Check counts, with partial credit for each check that passes. Hints are free; there are no solutions.
  • Quiz: 1 point a question, one attempt. Pick every answer, then Submit.
  • Sign in (top right) so your scores reach your instructor. Work saves as you go.
